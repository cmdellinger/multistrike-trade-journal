Multistrike Trade Journal Version: Toy

Welcome to the Multistrike Trade Journal!


This is a comprehensive trading journal for equities, foreign exchange, and cryptocurrencies. The goal is to track trade ideas and their outcomes with precision and insight.


A trade idea represents the execution of a trade setup, allowing multiple attempts with multiple entries and exits per attempt. This flexible structure helps you analyze your trading patterns and improve your strategy over time.


The Dashboard is currently under development and will feature cards with summaries and graphs of trade ideas outcomes. Stay tuned for updates!


This application is being built using Angular and Angular Material. As a learning project, it's evolving continuously with new features and improvements.
